# Prespecified retrospective development and new 2026 holdout

The original experiment on 2017–2025 data was observed and negative. Reuse those years only for retrospective development: train before 2021 and validate on 2021–2022; then train before 2023 and validate on 2023–2025. Pool validation squared errors. Freeze the search before execution: two, three or four HMM states; mixture strength 0, 0.25, 0.5 or 1; tree depth 1 with lambda 30, or depth 2 with lambda 10. Use 100 trees, learning rate 0.03, at most 30 HMM iterations and seed 42. Zero mixture strength is the matched global baseline. Also compare a zero forecast. Select minimum pooled validation RMSE, breaking ties toward less regime weighting, fewer states and shallower trees. Preserve all outcomes.

Freeze selection before fetching any 2026 Valet observations. Refit the selected configuration only on 2017–2025 labels. The new evaluation window is January 1 through October 14, 2026. At planning time, October 15 at 19:29 UTC (15:29 ET), that day's 16:30 ET publication was not yet safely available. These indicative daily averages are not executable closes. Original publication vintages are unavailable, limiting point-in-time revision analysis.

Require each training target date to precede the first evaluation feature date. Held-out probabilities use a causal forward filter; smoothing is limited to training. Advance through purged feature rows without fitting their labels. Persist the posterior and raw rolling context. Keep row-level forecasts under ignored data paths; commit only aggregate metrics, configurations and provenance. Evaluate the new 2026 window once after selection, compare both baselines, and report paired block-bootstrap uncertainty. Do not switch models or tune thresholds using test results.
